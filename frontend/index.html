How it works

From transaction evidence to an indicative estimate

1

Find relevant transactions

The service retrieves recent transactions for the same town and flat type.

2

Rank comparable flats

Transactions are scored using location, floor area, storey, flat model, remaining lease and recency.

3

Check evidence quality

An estimate is shown only when there are enough sufficiently similar transactions.

4

Interpret the result

Review the estimate, uncertainty range and comparable transactions together rather than relying on a single number.

Important

Indicative decision support, not an official valuation

The service is based on public HDB resale transaction data. Renovation quality, interior condition, orientation, exact unit characteristics and other unobserved factors may affect a flat's actual market value.

A difference between the asking price and the model estimate therefore does not by itself mean that a flat is overpriced or underpriced.
